# Staff Quick-Reference Guide: Smarter, Safer Passwords

Keeping your work accounts secure doesn't require memorizing complicated gibberish or changing your password every month. Follow these three simple steps to protect yourself and our organization.

---

### Step 1: Think Long, Not Complicated (Use a Passphrase)
Create a password that is **at least 12 characters long** by stringing together 3 to 4 random, unrelated words (spaces are allowed!).
* **Example:** `purple giraffe coffee bicycle`
* **Why:** A longer passphrase is easy for you to remember and type, yet takes modern computers centuries to guess—far stronger than `P@ssw0rd1!`.

---

### Step 2: Let Your Password Manager Do the Heavy Lifting
Save all your work logins inside our approved **Bitwarden Password Manager**, and let it generate unique, random passwords for every website you use.
* **Why:** You only ever need to remember **one** master passphrase. If one website suffers a data breach, attackers cannot use that leaked password to break into your email or company tools.

---

### Step 3: Turn On Two-Step Verification (2FA)
Always enable Two-Step Verification using an authenticator app or security key whenever prompted—especially for email, cloud apps, and financial tools.
* **Why:** Even if a hacker steals or guesses your password, they still cannot log in without the second verification code from your phone or security key.

---

### Quick Reminders
* **No more 30-day resets:** You will no longer be forced to change your password every month. Only change it if you suspect someone else knows it.
* **Never share or write down passwords:** Keep your master passphrase private and never send passwords over email or chat.
